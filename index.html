The emblem

Before there were clocks, there was Sírius

Sírius is the brightest star in the night sky. In ancient Egypt, its first rising before dawn announced the new year and the flooding of the Nile. It was one of humanity's first great timekeepers, and it is the star at the heart of our emblem.

  1. 8

    Eight points

    Four long rays for the cardinal directions, four shorter ones for the paths between them: the compass that navigators once read from the stars.

  2. 12

    Twelve hours

    Around the star, twelve marks. Four are drawn heavier, as on a dial, for twelve, three, six and nine o'clock.

  3. 2

    Two rings

    An outer ring for the case, a finer one for the bezel. The star sits where a dial would be.

  4. 1

    One point of light

    At the centre, the pivot every hand turns around. The star's light takes 8.6 years to reach us.

Frequently Asked

How is my watch delivered?

Every timepiece is shipped insured, in discreet packaging, and requires a signature on delivery. Tracking is sent by email.

Can I see a watch before buying?

Yes. Request a private viewing through our concierge and we will arrange a time with you.

How do I pay?

Pay securely by card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex and more), Apple Pay or Google Pay. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card details.

Can I return a watch?

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery, unworn, with its protective film, case and papers.

Will I pay duties or taxes?

Depending on your country, import duties or taxes may be charged on delivery. These are set by your local customs office.
